## What we do

Fife Data Meetup is a community-run group for people who work with data, or would like to. Each meetup has a couple of short talks followed by time to chat. It's free, informal and open to all.

## Where we meet

Venues will vary around Fife. Each [event page]({{ "/events/" | relative_url }}) lists the venue.
