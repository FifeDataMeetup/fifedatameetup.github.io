Fife Data Meetup is for everyone. By attending our events or joining our online spaces, you agree to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat others with kindness, whatever their background or experience.
- **Be inclusive.** Make room for different voices, and help newcomers feel welcome.
- **No harassment.** We won't tolerate harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- **Ask before photographing.** Check that people are happy to appear in photos you share.

If something makes you uncomfortable, speak to an organiser or email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Organisers may ask anyone who breaks this code to leave.
